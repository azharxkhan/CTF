# W1 — Port recon

**The setup:** a host runs several services on unusual high ports; one leaks a flag in its
banner, the rest are decoys.

**How to solve it:** scan for services and versions, then read the banner of the odd one.
```bash
nmap -sV <host> -p 47000-49999      # find open ports + identify the services
nc <host> 48219                     # read the banner — flag is the "token:" value
```
One decoy just says "nothing here"; `-sV` and a quick `nc` tell the real service from the noise.

**The lesson:** attackers enumerate before anything else. Services announce themselves in
banners — don't run things you don't need, and don't leak version/secret info in banners.
